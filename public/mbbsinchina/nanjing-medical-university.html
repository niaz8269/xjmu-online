About Nanjing Medical University

Nanjing Medical University (NMU), founded in 1934, is one of Jiangsu Province's most prestigious medical institutions. Located in the historic city of Nanjing, it offers comprehensive medical education with strong emphasis on clinical training and research. NMU is renowned for its extensive hospital network and excellent teaching facilities.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…
  • English medium MBBS program with comprehensive curriculum
  • Jiangsu Province's Key Medical University
  • Largest network of affiliated hospitals in Jiangsu
  • Strong focus on clinical skills and practical training
  • Historic location with modern educational facilities

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at Nanjing Medical University

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 25+ affiliated hospitals including The First Affiliated Hospital and Nanjing Jinling Hospital.

  • The First Affiliated Hospital (2800+ beds)
  • Nanjing Jinling Hospital
  • Nanjing Children's Hospital
  • Jiangsu Province Hospital

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at NMU.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 70% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • No criminal record
