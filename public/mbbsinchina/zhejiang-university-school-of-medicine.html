About Zhejiang University School of Medicine

Zhejiang University School of Medicine (ZJUSM), founded in 1912, is one of China's most prestigious medical institutions and a member of the elite C9 League. Located in the beautiful city of Hangzhou, it offers world-class medical education with a perfect blend of traditional excellence and modern innovation.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…
  • English medium MBBS program with international curriculum
  • C9 League University - China's Ivy League
  • State-of-the-art teaching hospitals and research centers
  • Strong international collaborations and exchange programs
  • Comprehensive clinical training and research opportunities

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at Zhejiang University School of Medicine

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 7 affiliated hospitals including Sir Run Run Shaw Hospital and The Second Affiliated Hospital.

  • Sir Run Run Shaw Hospital (1800+ beds)
  • The Second Affiliated Hospital
  • Children's Hospital
  • Women's Hospital

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at ZJUSM.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 75% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • No criminal record

Financial support programs for international students at Zhejiang University School of Medicine

Zhejiang Provincial Scholarship

30-70% Tuition Fee
  • 30-70% tuition fee discount
  • Available for outstanding students
  • Performance-based renewal
  • Separate application required

ZJU Outstanding Student Scholarship

20-50% Tuition Fee
  • 20-50% tuition fee waiver
  • Academic performance based

  • Automatic consideration

Important Notes:
