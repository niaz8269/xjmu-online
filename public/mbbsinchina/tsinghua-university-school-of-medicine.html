About Tsinghua University School of Medicine

Tsinghua University School of Medicine, established in 2001, represents China's most prestigious institution's commitment to medical excellence. As part of Tsinghua University - consistently ranked as China's top university - the medical school combines world-class research, innovative teaching methods, and exceptional clinical training in Beijing.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at Tsinghua University School of Medicine

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 8 affiliated hospitals including Tsinghua University Hospital and Beijing Tsinghua Changgung Hospital.

  • Tsinghua University Hospital (1900+ beds)
  • Beijing Tsinghua Changgung Hospital
  • Beijing United Family Hospital
  • International Medical Center

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at Tsinghua.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 90% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • No criminal record
