Are Chinese medical degrees recognized globally?

Yes, medical degrees from Chinese universities are recognized by WHO, MCI (Medical Council of India), and many other medical councils worldwide. However, it's important to check the specific recognition requirements of your home country.

What is the duration of the MBBS program in China?

The MBBS program in China typically lasts for 6 years, which includes 5 years of academic study and 1 year of internship. Some universities may offer a 5-year program that includes the internship within the curriculum.
