Xinjiang Medical University

  • Xinjiang Medical University (XJMU), founded in 1956 and located in the beautiful city of Urumqi, Xinjiang Province, is the largest and only provincial, ministerial joint medical university in Xinjiang. As a leading public government university, XJMU is well known for its modern teaching hospitals, advanced laboratories, and highly qualified faculty.
  • XJMU consists of 26 colleges offering 29 undergraduate majors, and currently hosts around 27,984 full-time students, including 21,682 undergraduates, 5,117 master’s candidates, 678 doctoral candidates, and 507 international students. The university’s academic trength is globally recognized Clinical Medicine ranks in the top 2.33‰ of ESI global rankings, while Pharmacology & Toxicology ranks in the top 8.32‰, reflecting its strong research capability. Biology and Biochemistry disciplines also show excellent growth potential with a rise to 91.43%
  • XJMU started enrolling international students in 1993 and is one of the 44 MOE-listed medical universities in China authorized to accept self-funded international students for the English-taught MBBS program. Over the past 30 years, the university has trained more than 2,300 international students, including 160+ postgraduates, 1,800+ undergraduates, and 345+ trainees in various fields such as Clinical Medicine, Integrative Chinese & Western Medicine, Traditional Chinese Medicine, Stomatology, Acupuncture & Moxibustion, Public Health, Preventive Medicine, and Pharmacy.
  • The MBBS program is fully taught in English, with core medical subjects starting from the first year. HSK4 is required only at the time of graduation, not for initial admission. XJMU is especially popular among Pakistani students due to its geographic convenience, only a 2 hr 50 min direct flight from Islamabad or 20–25 hours by road from the Pakistan border.
  • Xinjiang Medical University is recognized by WHO (WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, and other global medical authorities, making graduates fully eligible to sit for USMLE, FMGE, NExT, PMDC NRE, and other international licensing exams.ls

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 8 affiliated hospitals with modern medical equipment and experienced faculty.

  • First Affiliated Hospital (2000+ beds)
  • Second Affiliated Hospital
  • Tumor Hospital

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at XJMU.
