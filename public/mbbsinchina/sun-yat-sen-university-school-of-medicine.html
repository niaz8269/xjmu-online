About Sun Yat-sen University School of Medicine

Sun Yat-sen University School of Medicine (SYSU Med), established in 1866, is one of China's oldest and most prestigious medical institutions. As a Double First-Class University located in Guangzhou - Southern China's economic hub - it offers world-class medical education with strong clinical training and international recognition.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…
  • English medium MBBS program with international curriculum
  • Double First-Class University - China's Top-tier Program
  • Extensive network of affiliated teaching hospitals
  • Strong international collaborations and research programs
  • Excellent location in China's southern economic center

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at Sun Yat-sen University School of Medicine

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 12+ affiliated hospitals including The First Affiliated Hospital and Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital.

  • The First Affiliated Hospital (2300+ beds)
  • Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital
  • Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center
  • Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at SYSU Med.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 75% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • No criminal record
