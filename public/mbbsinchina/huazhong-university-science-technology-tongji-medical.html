About HUST Tongji Medical College

Huazhong University of Science and Technology Tongji Medical College (HUST Tongji), founded in 1907, is one of China's oldest and most prestigious medical institutions. As a Project 985 university located in Wuhan, it combines century-old medical traditions with modern educational approaches, offering exceptional value for international medical students.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…
  • English medium MBBS program with comprehensive curriculum
  • Project 985 University - Chinese Government's Elite Program
  • Large network of affiliated teaching hospitals
  • Strong international student community and support
  • Excellent cost-to-quality ratio for medical education

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at HUST Tongji Medical College

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 10+ affiliated hospitals including Tongji Hospital and Union Hospital.

  • Tongji Hospital (2500+ beds)

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at HUST Tongji.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 70% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate

Scholarship Opportunities

Financial support programs for international students at HUST Tongji Medical College

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Free university dormitory
  • Monthly living allowance (2,500 CNY)
  • Comprehensive medical insurance

Hubei Provincial Scholarship

40-70% Tuition Fee
  • 40-70% tuition fee discount
  • Available for meritorious students
  • Performance-based renewal
  • Separate application required
Eligibility: 75%+ in HSSC & good academic record

HUST Tongji Merit Scholarship

25-50% Tuition Fee
  • 25-50% tuition fee waiver
  • Academic performance based
  • Available each academic year
  • Automatic consideration
Eligibility: Top 25% of admitted students

Important Notes:

  • Scholarship applications must be submitted with admission application
  • Documents required: Academic transcripts, recommendation letters, personal statement
  • Scholarship results announced with admission decision
  • Maintain GPA 2.8+ to renew scholarships annually
