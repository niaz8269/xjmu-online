About Tianjin Medical University

Tianjin Medical University (TMU), founded in 1951, is one of Northern China's leading medical institutions. Located in Tianjin - a major port city and municipality directly under the central government - TMU offers comprehensive medical education with strong clinical training programs and modern research facilities.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…
  • English medium MBBS program with international curriculum
  • Tianjin Municipal Key University
  • Modern teaching hospitals with advanced facilities
  • Strong international collaborations and student exchange
  • Strategic location in Northern China's economic center

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at Tianjin Medical University

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 15+ affiliated hospitals including TMU General Hospital and Tianjin Cancer Hospital.

  • TMU General Hospital (2000+ beds)
  • Tianjin Cancer Hospital
  • Tianjin Children's Hospital
  • Tianjin Chest Hospital

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at TMU.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 70% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • No criminal record
