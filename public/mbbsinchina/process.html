Eligibility Requirements

Check if you meet the basic eligibility criteria for MBBS programs in Chinese universities

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum 70% in Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • High School Diploma or equivalent
  • Age between 17-25 years
  • NEET qualification (for Indian students)

Language Requirements

  • Medium of instruction: English
  • No IELTS/TOEFL required
  • Basic English proficiency
  • Chinese language course during MBBS

Medical Requirements

  • Good physical and mental health
  • No contagious diseases
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • HIV/AIDS test report
