About Peking University Health Science Center

Peking University Health Science Center (PKUHSC), founded in 1912, is one of China's most prestigious and oldest medical institutions. Located in Beijing, it consistently ranks among the top medical universities in China and Asia, offering world-class medical education and research opportunities.

Key Features

  • Recognized by WHO(WDOMS), ECFMG, PMDC, BMDC, NMC, SCHS, HPCSA, USMLE, WCAME…
  • English medium MBBS program with international curriculum
  • World-class faculty and research facilities
  • State-of-the-art teaching hospitals
  • Global partnerships and exchange programs
  • Extensive clinical training opportunities

MBBS Program Details

Comprehensive information about the MBBS program at Peking University Health Science Center

Curriculum

The MBBS program follows international standards with comprehensive medical education including basic sciences, clinical medicine, and practical training.

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects (2 years)
  • Para-Clinical Subjects (1.5 years)
  • Clinical Subjects (2.5 years)
  • Internship (1 year)

Clinical Training

Students get extensive clinical exposure in 12 affiliated hospitals including Peking University First Hospital and Peking University People's Hospital.

  • Peking University First Hospital (2000+ beds)
  • Peking University People's Hospital
  • Peking University Third Hospital
  • Peking University Stomatological Hospital

Eligibility

Admission requirements for international students seeking MBBS at PKUHSC.

  • Age: 18-25 years
  • Minimum 85% in HSSC/A-Level
  • Valid passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • No criminal record
