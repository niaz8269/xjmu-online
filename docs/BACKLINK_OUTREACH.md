# Backlink plan for xjmu.online (22 November 2026)

Goal: get other websites to link to xjmu.online, which is the main gap between this site and the top results for
"MBBS in China".

## What was checked, and what was not

- **Checked directly:** all 67 main and news pages of cas.moe plus its Xinjiang Medical University page. **None links
  to xjmu.online.** (xjmu.online links to cas.moe from its guides; nothing comes back.)
- **Checked directly:** universitiespage.com publishes a guest-post invitation; studyabroad.pk has an "Advertise"
  page and a consultants directory.
- **Not available:** competitors' backlink lists. The Ahrefs plan does not include backlink reports and the Semrush
  account has no units left. The outside sites below come from web searches, not from a competitor link audit.
  If either account is topped up, re-run this for timesconsultant.com, eaziline.com and nxmuni.com.

## Step 2: your own profiles (free, one hour)

Put https://xjmu.online/ in the website field of every profile you already have: Google Business Profile,
Facebook page, Instagram, YouTube channel (and in each video description), LinkedIn company page, TikTok.
Most of these do not pass ranking strength, but Google uses them to confirm the site is a real organisation.

## Step 3: outside sites

| Site | What to ask for | Status |
|---|---|---|
| universitiespage.com | A guest article. Their page says: at least 600 words, original, with headings; "specific university guidance" and "visa guidelines" are accepted topics. Send to the email shown on universitiespage.com/guides/write-us-guest-post-for-study-abroad- | Confirmed they invite guest posts |
| studyabroad.pk | A consultant listing for CAS with the website link, through their "Advertise" / consultants section | Listing exists; price and link policy not confirmed |
| ilmkidunya.com, eduvision.edu.pk | Ask whether they take a contributed article or a university profile | Not confirmed; both have MBBS-in-China pages |
| dev.to, hashnode.com, issuu.com, medium.com, LinkedIn articles | Publish your own short article (500 to 700 new words, not a copy of a guide) that links to the matching guide | Free, self-service. Competitors' articles on these sites already appear in Google. Links are usually "nofollow": they bring readers and AI citations more than ranking strength |
| Quora, Reddit (r/MBBS, r/pakistan, study-abroad groups) | Answer real questions fully; link a guide only where it answers the question | Free; helps AI Overviews; never post the same text twice |
| Your own students | Ask students or graduates with a blog or YouTube channel to mention the site | Strongest kind of link; ask five people |

Do not buy link packages or "500 backlinks" offers. Google ignores or penalises them, and the site is new enough
that a penalty would undo this month's work. Aim for two to four real links a month.

## How to tell it is working

Search Console shows new linking sites under Links > Top linking sites, usually two to four weeks after a link
goes live. Check once a month.
