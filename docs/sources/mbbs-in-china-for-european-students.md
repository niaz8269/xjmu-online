## Facts taken from the owner or already published on this site (not re-checked against external pages)

- XJMU: minimum 60% in each of physics, chemistry and biology (A-level/HSSC equivalent); age 18 to 35; March/Spring intake and September/Fall intake; ECFMG eligible; HSK level 4 required by graduation; no CSCA test; fees for 2027 as 2026 (¥49,950 first year, ¥35,950 later, about ¥229,700 over six years, about USD 7,000 / 5,050 / 32,000); 18 teaching hospitals and 95 internship hospitals (owner-confirmed).
- MOE notice 教外司来〔2026〕94号 of 1 June 2026: 43 universities, 2,790 seats, XJMU number 8 with 60 seats; the five rules (published in `moe-listed-mbbs-universities-in-china`).
- XJMU in the World Directory of Medical Schools (published in `who-listed-medical-universities-in-china`; not re-checked in WDOMS during this session, since the directory's search is script driven). The GMC page does state that the awarding institution must have a WDMS entry.
- Application steps (¥1,000 fee, ¥2,000 refundable deposit, Pre-Admission Letter, JW202), documents list, admission calendar, living costs, scholarships: from `_shared.php` and the visa guide.
- The USD column of the fee table keeps the USD figures already published on the site. At the ECB rates above, ¥49,950 would be about USD 7,500, so the published USD figures are not recalculated; the guide says so in the text.
