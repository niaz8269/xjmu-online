## Fee table arithmetic

Yuan fee x rate, rounded to the nearest 100: 49,950 -> USD 7,494 / CAD 10,714; 35,950 -> USD 5,394 / CAD 7,711; 229,700 -> USD 34,464 / CAD 49,271. Note: other pages on this site state "about USD 7,000 / 5,050 / 32,000" (an earlier, rounder conversion, about ¥1 = USD 0.14). The new guide's USD figures are higher because the Bank of Canada cross rate on 24 Nov 2026 gives about USD 0.15 per yuan. The guide says so in the text. Owner may wish to refresh the USD figures site-wide.
