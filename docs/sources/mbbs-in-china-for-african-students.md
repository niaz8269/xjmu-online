## Facts taken from the owner's confirmed list or already published on this site (not re-verified externally)

- XJMU requirements (60% in each of physics, chemistry, biology; age 18-35), intakes, ECFMG eligible, HSK 4 by graduation, fees (49,950 / 35,950 / about 229,700 yuan), no CSCA, 18 teaching and 95 internship hospitals, MOE list 2026-2027 (43 universities, 2,790 seats, XJMU number 8 with 60 seats), calendar dates, application steps and fees, scholarships table, living costs: owner-confirmed or in resources/guides/_shared.php.
- A non-listed university may teach international students clinical medicine only in Chinese and may not issue an English-language MBBS certificate: from resources/guides/_answers.php and the non-moe-universities guide (the site's reading of MOE notice 教外司来〔2026〕94号).
- XJMU listed in WDOMS: stated in resources/guides/_answers.php. Not re-checked at wdoms.org; the guide tells readers to check.
