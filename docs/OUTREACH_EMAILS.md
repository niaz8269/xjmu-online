# Outreach drafts (prepared 27 Nov 2026, nothing has been sent)

Goal: real links to xjmu.online from other sites, to lift the position of the non-brand searches ("MBBS in China 2027" and similar).
Bing shows 10 referring domains for xjmu.online against 112 for Timesconsultant and 48 for Eaziline (see BACKLINK_OUTREACH.md).

Rules for every message
- Send from the admissions address (info@xjmu.online), signed with a real name. Never pay for a link, and never offer a gift in exchange.
- State only facts that are already on xjmu.online (MOE list of 1 June 2026: 43 universities, 2,790 seats, XJMU number 8 with 60 seats; fees unchanged for 2027: first-year package ¥49,950, then ¥35,950 a year; March/Spring and September/Fall intakes).
- Do not write that any council "recognises" XJMU in a message to a site that will publish it. Let the guides say what is checked.
- Record every send in the tracking table at the bottom, and follow up once after 10 days. Do not send a third message.

## A. Sites that publish guest articles or listings (best first)

### 1. universitiespage.com: guest article
- **Verified:** their page "Write Us Guest Post for Study Abroad" (https://universitiespage.com/guides/write-us-guest-post-for-study-abroad-) asks for articles of at least 600 words, original, with headings, on study-abroad guidance, specific university or course guidance, scholarships and visas. Send to **info@universitiespage.com** (read from the page).
- **Article to offer (write it new, 700 to 900 words, do not copy a guide):** "MBBS in China 2027: a 10-point checklist before you pay any deposit" (is the university on the Ministry of Education list, what the X1 visa needs, what the first-year fee covers, what to ask your own medical council, how to spot a fake admission letter). One link in the text to https://xjmu.online/mbbs-in-china-eligibility-2027 and one to https://xjmu.online/non-moe-universities-in-china-mbbs-risks.

> Subject: Guest article for UniversitiesPage: MBBS in China 2027 checklist
>
> Hello,
>
> I handle international admissions for Xinjiang Medical University. I read your guest post guidelines (600 words or more, original, with headings) and would like to send you an article titled "MBBS in China 2027: a 10-point checklist before you pay any deposit".
>
> It is written for students and parents in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh and Africa, uses only published facts (the Ministry of Education list of 1 June 2026, visa rules and fee structures) and has not been published elsewhere. It would contain two links to our own guides as sources.
>
> May I send the draft?
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name]
> International Students Admissions, Xinjiang Medical University
> https://xjmu.online/ | info@xjmu.online

### 2. studyabroad.pk: consultant or advertiser listing
- **Verified:** https://www.studyabroad.pk/advertise exists and the site has a consultants directory; there is no public email on the page. Use the form on that page.
- **What to ask for:** a listing for Consultancy for Abroad Study (CAS) with the website field set to https://xjmu.online/ and a short text.

> Hello,
>
> We would like to list Consultancy for Abroad Study (CAS) in your consultants directory. CAS handles admissions to Xinjiang Medical University (Urumqi, China) and other MOE listed universities for Pakistani students. Please tell me the listing options, the cost if any, and whether the listing can carry our website, https://xjmu.online/.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name], Consultancy for Abroad Study (CAS)

### 3. apkavisa.pk: free business listing
- **Verified:** https://apkavisa.pk/list-your-business has "free", "featured" and "sponsored" tiers (the page text did not load for me, so read the free-tier terms before you submit). Their contact page is a WhatsApp number and a form.
- **Use only the free tier** for Consultancy for Abroad Study (CAS), category study abroad, website https://xjmu.online/. Ask nothing else.

### 4. ilmkidunya.com and eduvision.edu.pk: article or profile
- **Verified:** both publish MBBS in China pages. I could not find a public contact address for either; use the contact form on each site.
- **Ask:** whether they accept a short, sourced university profile or a contributed article. Same short message as item 1, naming their site.
