## 4. Changes made in this round

- Every guide now opens with a 40–60 word **Quick answer** and a **Key facts** table (degree, duration, language,
  MOE status and seats, intakes, eligibility, fees, six-year total). These are the passages AI Overviews and featured
  snippets lift.
- BlogPosting schema with abstract, datePublished, image and `about: Xinjiang Medical University`, plus Speakable.
- Guides listed on /blogs as "MBBS in China 2027 Guides" and given Blog breadcrumbs. **URLs unchanged.**
- "Clinical Medicine in China" added (FAQ and keywords on /mbbs-in-china-2027).
- Remaining "spring 2027" wording changed to "March/Spring 2027".

## 5. What moves "MBBS in China" from here (in order)

1. **Links from other sites (owner).** This is the gap between us and positions 1–4. Get links from:
   - the university's official site
   - education directories and Pakistani or Indian education blogs and news
   - student forums and Facebook groups (links from those are weaker but bring visits)
   - partner consultancies
   - a Google Business Profile for the admissions office
2. **Confirmed 2027 facts (owner).** 2027 fees, deadlines, the next MOE list, and approximate fees in PKR and INR with
   the date of the exchange rate. Times Consultant wins on PKR; no competitor gives INR.
3. **More guides (developer):**
   - /clinical-medicine-in-china
   - /who-listed-medical-universities-in-china
   - /xinjiang-medical-university (ranking and recognition hub)
   - /after-mbbs-in-china (USMLE, PLAB, NRE, FMGE)
4. **A named adviser byline** with credentials on the guides (owner to supply a real person). No competitor has one.
5. **Weekly:** review Search Console queries at positions 5–15 and strengthen the page that ranks for each.
   Request indexing after every content change.
6. **Admin-panel fields (owner):** `intake_year`, `mbbs_facts_list`, `mbbs_cta_lead`, eligibility 60% and age 18–35
   (see SEO_KEYWORDS.md).

No one can promise position 1; these are the levers that decide it.
